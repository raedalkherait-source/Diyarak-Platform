# ADR-0007: Keep Platform Listing sector-agnostic

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

`Diyarak.Platform.Listing` owns reusable platform listing capabilities and must remain independent of business modules such as `Diyarak.Market.Property`.

Market-specific property data remains owned by `Diyarak.Market.Property`.

A listing may identify its published subject only through a sector-agnostic contract that does not make Platform Core depend on a business module.

The concrete subject-reference contract and listing lifecycle remain deferred until their requirements are defined.
## Follow-up

ADR-0010 resolves the previously deferred concrete sector-agnostic subject-reference contract.

Listing lifecycle requirements remain deferred.
