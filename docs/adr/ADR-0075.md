# ADR-0075: Off-Plan Projects

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Developers and agencies in the Syrian real estate market commonly sell units in projects before
construction is complete (بيع على المخطط). The platform needs a way for agencies to list upcoming
projects with unit count, price range and expected completion date, so buyers can discover and
reserve units early.

## Decision

- Add an `OffPlanProject` aggregate to `Diyarak.Market.Company`, owned by an `Agency`.
- Fields: name, description, governorate, district, construction status (Planned, UnderConstruction,
  NearCompletion), total units, USD price range (min/max), expected completion date.
- Projects start as unpublished drafts; the agency owner publishes and unpublishes them.
  Only unpublished projects can be deleted.
- API: authenticated CRUD at `/api/market/offplan`, public list and detail at
  `/api/market/public/offplan` (filterable by governorate, paginated).
- Portal: public `/projects` list page with governorate filter and `/projects/{id}` detail page;
  agency management at `/my/projects`.
- A "مشاريع" link in the main navigation header.

## Consequences

- Agencies can market off-plan projects alongside regular listings.
- The public can browse projects by governorate and see developer (agency) details.
- Future iterations can add unit-level detail, floor plans, and reservation payments.
