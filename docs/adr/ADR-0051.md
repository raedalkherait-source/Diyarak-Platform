# ADR-0051: Syrian market Property details

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The Property aggregate was modelled on a German-style address (street, house number and postal
code all required) and had no way to express details that buyers and renters in Syria filter on:
the governorate and district, the legal ownership status, the finishing level, the floor, and
local utilities such as solar power or a generator subscription.

## Decision

Address:

- `PropertyAddress.City` stays required. `Street`, `HouseNumber` and `PostalCode` become optional;
  when supplied they must be non-blank.
- New optional `Governorate` (`SyrianGovernorate`, the 14 governorates) and `District`
  (neighbourhood, free text, non-blank when supplied).

Property:

- `LegalTitleType`: `GreenTitleDeed`, `AgriculturalTitleDeed`, `TitleDeedShares`, `CourtRuling`,
  `NotarizedPowerOfAttorney`, `HousingCooperativeAllocation`, `KeyMoneyTenancy`.
- `FinishingLevel`: `Unfinished`, `PartiallyFinished`, `Standard`, `Deluxe`, `SuperDeluxe`.
- `FloorNumber` (`-5`–`200`, `0` = ground floor, negative = basement) and `BuildingFloorCount`
  (`1`–`200`); a floor cannot exceed the building floor count.
- New `PropertyFeature` values: `SolarPowerSystem`, `GeneratorSubscription`, `WaterTank`,
  `WaterWell`, `CentralHeating`, `AirConditioning`, `Furnished`, `SecurityGuard`.

All new values are optional and follow the existing full-replacement semantics of
`PUT /api/market/properties/{propertyId}`: omitted values are cleared.

HTTP:

- Create/replace requests accept `address.governorate`, `address.district`, `legalTitleType`,
  `finishingLevel`, `floorNumber` and `buildingFloorCount`; `street`, `houseNumber` and
  `postalCode` may be omitted.
- Management and public responses expose the new fields. Public responses expose governorate and
  district but still never the street or house number.
- The public collection accepts the filters `governorate` (repeatable), `district`
  (case-insensitive exact match), `legalTitleType` (repeatable), `finishingLevel` (repeatable),
  and `floorMin`/`floorMax`.

Persistence:

- Migration `MarketPropertySyrianMarketDetails` makes `street`, `house_number` and `postal_code`
  nullable and adds nullable `governorate`, `district`, `legal_title_type`, `finishing_level`,
  `floor_number` and `building_floor_count` columns. Existing rows are unchanged. The down
  migration back-fills empty strings before restoring `NOT NULL`.

## Consequences

Listings can be created with the address information that actually exists in Syria and searched
by the attributes local users care about. Existing German-style data remains valid. Governorate
is optional at the domain level so legacy rows keep loading; client applications should require
it for new listings. Search indexes for the new filters are deferred until query volume justifies
them.
