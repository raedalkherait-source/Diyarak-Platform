# ADR-0078: User Reviews for Contractors and Agencies

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Users need a way to evaluate contractors and agencies they have worked with. Social proof through
reviews and ratings helps other users make informed decisions.

## Decision

- Add a `Review` aggregate to `Diyarak.Market.Company` with: author user, subject (contractor or
  agency), rating (1–5 stars), optional comment (max 1000 characters).
- One review per user per subject (unique constraint on author + subject).
- Users cannot review their own contractor or agency profile.
- Reviews are immutable after creation; authors can delete their own.
- API: authenticated create/delete at `/api/market/reviews`, public list and average at
  `/api/market/public/reviews`.
- Portal: review form and rating display on contractor cards and agency pages.

## Consequences

- Users can rate contractors and agencies, building trust in the marketplace.
- The one-review-per-subject constraint prevents spam while keeping feedback meaningful.
- Future iterations can add moderation of reviews and display on agency detail pages.
