# ADR-0057: Listing lifecycle after publication

## Status

Accepted. Supersedes the Draft-only editing rule of ADR-0029, and the Draft-only photo and contact
rules of ADR-0053 and ADR-0055.

## Context

Listings could not change once published: no price drop, no new photo, no new contact
preference. Publishers also had no way to hide a listing temporarily or mark a property as sold
or rented. Real publishers need all of these daily.

## Decision

Lifecycle (`Diyarak.Market.Listing`):

```
Draft --publish--> Published --pause--> Paused
                   Published <--resume-- Paused
         Published | Paused --close(reason)--> Closed
```

- New statuses `Paused` (3) and `Closed` (4). `Closed` records a `ListingClosureReason`: `Sold`,
  `Rented` or `Withdrawn`. Closed is terminal.
- Context, headline, price, availability date, contact options and photos can change in
  `Draft`, `Published` and `Paused`, but not in `Closed`. Setters never clear required
  publication data, so an edited Published listing always stays ready for publication.
- Only `Published` listings are publicly visible. Public detail, collection, photo and contact
  routes treat `Paused` and `Closed` exactly like missing listings (`404`).

Application and HTTP:

- `ChangeListingStatusUseCase` applies pause, resume and close for the creator only (concealed
  `404` otherwise). It uses the existing transaction runner and optimistic version check, and
  returns the next version.
- `POST /api/market/listings/{id}/pause`, `/resume`, `/close` (`{ "reason": "Sold" | "Rented" |
  "Withdrawn" }`) → `200 { listingId, version }`. Invalid transitions return
  `409 market.listing_status.invalid_transition`. A missing or unknown reason returns `400`.
- `PATCH /api/market/listings/{id}` now works on Published and Paused listings. `409
  market.listing.cannot_edit` applies only to Closed listings.
- Management responses include `closureReason`.
- Public ETags now include the ordered photo identifiers (representation revision v3), because
  photos can change without a Listing version change.

Persistence:

- `market.listings.closure_reason` with the check constraint
  `(status = 4) = (closure_reason IS NOT NULL)` (migration `MarketListingLifecycle`). Rehydration
  replays the lifecycle through the aggregate (publish, then pause or close), so invariants
  hold.

Portal:

- The listing management page shows an edit form for Published and Paused listings (offer,
  headline, price, contact), photo management until closed, a pause or resume button, and a
  confirmed "end listing" section. That section offers "Sold" or "Rented", depending on the
  offer, plus "Withdraw". Status badges in "My listings" show the closure reason.
- Edits and photo changes invalidate the cached public page immediately.

## Consequences

Publishers can keep listings accurate and end them honestly, and buyers stop seeing sold
properties. Closed listings remain in the database for the publisher's history and future
statistics. Editing property details (area, rooms, and so on) from the portal, deleting Drafts,
automatic expiry of stale listings, and keeping public "sold" pages for SEO are open.
