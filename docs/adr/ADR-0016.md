# ADR-0016: Validate Listing subject availability before publication

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Publishing a `Diyarak.Market.Listing.Listing` requires the referenced subject to exist and be available for publication.

This validation belongs to application-level publication orchestration and must occur before calling `Listing.Publish()`.

The `Listing` aggregate remains responsible for its own domain state and publication-readiness invariants. It does not resolve or load the referenced subject itself.

`Diyarak.Market.Listing` must not introduce a direct dependency on `Diyarak.Market.Property` to perform this validation.

The exact application contract, resolver boundary, cross-module communication mechanism, and definition of subject availability remain separate decisions.

This decision does not define behavior for a subject that becomes unavailable after a Listing has already been published.
