# ADR-0058: Listing reports and moderation

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Fraudulent and misleading listings, such as fake low prices or demands for payment before a
viewing, are the main trust risk for a property marketplace in Syria. Visitors need a way to
flag them. The platform needs a small trusted team that can hide them quickly without deleting
the publisher's data.

## Decision

Domain (`Diyarak.Market.Listing`):

- New status `Suspended` (5) with a required `ModerationNote` of up to 500 characters, shown to
  the publisher. Only Published or Paused listings can be suspended. `Reinstate()` returns the
  listing to Published and clears the note. While suspended, the publisher may edit or withdraw
  (close) the listing but cannot pause or resume it. Suspended listings are not publicly visible.
- `ListingReport`: listing, reason (`FraudOrScam`, `IncorrectInformation`,
  `AlreadySoldOrRented`, `Duplicate`, `InappropriateContent`, `Other`), an optional comment of up
  to 500 characters, and the creation time. A report is resolved once, as `Dismissed` or
  `ListingSuspended`, with the moderator and time recorded.

Application (`Diyarak.Market.Application.Moderation`):

- `ReportListingUseCase`: anyone can report a publicly visible listing, capped at 20 stored
  reports per listing per hour.
- `ListModerationQueueUseCase`: open reports grouped by listing, oldest first.
- `ModerateListingUseCase` runs in one transaction. Suspend changes the listing and resolves all
  of its open reports as `ListingSuspended`. Reinstate changes the listing back. Dismiss resolves
  the open reports as `Dismissed`.
- `ListSuspendedListingsUseCase` and `GetListingForModerationUseCase`.

Authorization:

- Moderators are configured by verified phone number: `Moderation:ModeratorPhoneNumbers` (E.164,
  validated at startup). The `moderator` policy resolves the token's external identity to a user,
  looks up that user's verified phone (ADR-0052), and succeeds only on a configured number. With
  no numbers configured, nobody is a moderator. Role management in the database is open work.

HTTP:

- `POST /api/market/public/listings/{id}/reports` `{ reason, comment? }` → `202`, anonymous, with
  its own per-client rate limit (`RateLimiting:Report`, default 10 per hour).
- `/api/moderation/*` (moderators only, `no-store`): `GET queue`, `GET suspended`,
  `GET listings/{id}`, `POST listings/{id}/suspend { note }`, `POST listings/{id}/reinstate`,
  `POST listings/{id}/dismiss-reports`.
- Management responses include `moderationNote`.

Persistence:

- `market.listings.moderation_note`, with check `(status = 5) = (moderation_note IS NOT NULL)`.
- `market.listing_reports`, with check constraints on resolution consistency and comment length,
  an index on `(listing_id, created_at)`, and a partial index on open reports (migration
  `MarketListingModeration`).

Portal:

- Listing pages have a collapsible "report this listing" form, which goes through a same-origin
  route that forwards the client address.
- `/admin` shows the open-report queue with the reason, time and comment, plus actions to suspend
  (with a note for the publisher) or dismiss. It also lists suspended listings with a reinstate
  action. Non-moderators see a refusal message.
- The publisher's management page explains a suspension, shows the moderator's note, and offers
  to withdraw the listing.

## Consequences

Visitors can flag bad listings in two taps, and moderators can hide them immediately while
keeping a full record. Open work: moderator roles stored in the database, an audit log of
moderator actions, notifying publishers by SMS or WhatsApp, optional pre-publication review, and
a link to `/admin` in the navigation for moderators.
