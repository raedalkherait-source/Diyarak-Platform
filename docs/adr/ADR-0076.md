# ADR-0076: Area Price Reports

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Prospective buyers and renters in Syria want to understand market price levels before making
decisions. The platform already has published listings with known prices, governorate and district
data. Aggregating these into area-level price reports gives visitors a quick overview without
browsing individual listings.

## Decision

- Add an `IAreaPriceReportQuery` read-only query interface in `Diyarak.Market.Application.PriceReports`
  that aggregates published listing prices by governorate (summary) or by district (drill-down).
- Results are grouped by area, transaction intent (Sell/Rent) and property category.
- A minimum of 3 listings per group is required to avoid exposing individual pricing.
- PostgreSQL implementation joins `market.listings` with `market.properties` and uses `GROUP BY`
  with `AVG`, `MIN`, `MAX`, `COUNT` aggregations.
- API: `GET /api/market/public/price-reports` (governorate summary),
  `GET /api/market/public/price-reports/{governorate}` (district breakdown). Both anonymous.
- Portal: `/prices` page with governorate chips; clicking a governorate shows district-level data.
- An "أسعار" (Prices) link in the main navigation header.

## Consequences

- Visitors can compare price levels across governorates and districts.
- Reports are real-time from published listings, not cached snapshots — small datasets will show
  gaps where fewer than 3 listings exist.
- Future iterations can add time-series trends and per-square-meter normalization.
