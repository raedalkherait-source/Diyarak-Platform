# ADR-0010: Define a sector-agnostic listing subject reference

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

`Diyarak.Platform.Listing` will own a sector-agnostic `ListingSubjectReference` contract.

The reference will identify a published subject using:

- a `Guid` subject identifier;
- an opaque string subject type.

Platform Listing must not define a business-sector enum for subject types and must not reference business modules.

Business modules own the meaning of their subject-type values.

`Diyarak.Market.Listing` is explicitly approved to reference `Diyarak.Platform.Listing` for the listing subject-reference contract.

The initial Diyarak Market use case is publishing a Property. This decision does not introduce support for additional Market subject types before concrete requirements exist.

Subject existence validation, subject resolution, deletion behavior, persistence mapping, transport representation, and listing lifecycle behavior remain separate decisions.
