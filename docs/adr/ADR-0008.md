# ADR-0008: Keep market-specific listing behavior in Market

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Market-specific listing concepts and behavior belong to `Diyarak.Market.Listing`.

`Diyarak.Platform.Listing` remains sector-agnostic and owns only reusable platform listing capabilities.

`Diyarak.Market.Listing` must not depend directly on another business module implementation such as `Diyarak.Market.Property`.

The concrete sector-agnostic subject-reference contract remains deferred until its requirements are defined.
## Follow-up

ADR-0010 resolves the previously deferred concrete sector-agnostic subject-reference contract and explicitly approves `Diyarak.Market.Listing` to reference `Diyarak.Platform.Listing` for that contract.

Listing lifecycle and other Market-specific listing behavior remain subject to concrete requirements.
