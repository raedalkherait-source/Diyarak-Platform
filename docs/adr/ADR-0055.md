# ADR-0055: Contacting the publisher

## Status

Accepted

## Context

A listing is useless if buyers and tenants cannot reach the publisher. In Syria people expect to
call or use WhatsApp. Publishing raw phone numbers in listing pages invites mass scraping and
spam. Letting publishers type an arbitrary number would let anyone advertise someone else's
number.

## Decision

- A Listing has optional `ListingContactOptions` (`AllowCalls`, `AllowWhatsApp`, with at least one
  channel allowed). As with other publication data, it can be set or cleared only while the
  Listing is a Draft, via `PATCH /api/market/listings/{id}` with
  `"contact": { "allowCalls": bool, "allowWhatsApp": bool }` or `"contact": null`.
- The Listing never stores a phone number. The number used is always the publisher's verified
  phone number from phone sign-in (ADR-0052), looked up through the Market-owned port
  `IPublisherPhoneLookup`. Enabling contact without a verified phone returns
  `409 market.listing.contact_phone_not_verified`.
- Public Listing responses expose only which channels exist (`contact: { allowCalls,
  allowWhatsApp }` or `null`), never the number.
- `GET /api/market/public/listings/{id}/contact` reveals `{ phoneNumber, callUrl, whatsAppUrl }`
  for Published Listings whose publisher opted in. Otherwise it returns `404`. Responses are
  `Cache-Control: no-store` and `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`. A separate per-client rate
  limit applies (`RateLimiting:Contact`, default 30 per 15 minutes).
- Columns `contact_allow_calls` / `contact_allow_whatsapp` on `market.listings` (migration
  `MarketListingContactOptions`), with a check constraint that both are null or both are set
  with at least one true.
- Per-client limits behind a proxy: `ReverseProxy:KnownProxies` (IP list) enables
  `X-Forwarded-For` handling, but only from those addresses and only for one hop. The portal
  forwards the right-most `X-Forwarded-For` entry, which is the address added by its nearest
  proxy. Client-supplied entries to its left are not trusted.
- Portal: listing pages show a "show contact number" button. The number is fetched on click
  through a same-origin route (`/api/contact/{id}`), so it is not present in the server-rendered
  HTML. The WhatsApp link carries a pre-filled Arabic message naming the listing. On phones the
  price and contact block sits directly under the title.

## Consequences

Interested people can call or message the publisher in one tap. Numbers cannot be harvested from
listing pages, and only verified numbers can be advertised. Publishers who sign in through
external OIDC without a phone account cannot offer contact until they verify a phone.
In-platform messaging, call tracking, and per-listing alternative numbers (for example an
agency switchboard) remain open.
