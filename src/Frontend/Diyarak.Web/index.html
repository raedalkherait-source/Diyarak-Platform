Matches the full postal code, ignoring letter case. Leading zeros and internal spaces are preserved.

Selecting a commercial type searches commercial properties. Use Any category or Commercial property; another category may return no results.

Price ranges show listings with a known price in the selected currency. Enter a three-letter currency code, such as SYP, USD, or EUR.

Enter at least one living area bound and choose its unit. The API compares areas across units; listings without a known living area are excluded.

Total rooms may include fractions. Bedrooms and bathrooms must be whole numbers. Each range accepts one or both bounds and excludes listings missing that count.

Construction years start at 1; parking spaces may be 0. Use whole numbers and one or both bounds. Listings missing the filtered value are excluded.
